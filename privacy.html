Privacy Policy — Personal Calendar Sync

Last updated: September 30, 2026

What this app is

Personal Calendar Sync is a private OAuth client used only by its owner to connect the owner's Google Calendar to the TaskNotes plugin in the owner's own Obsidian vault.

What Google user data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Google Calendar data: the list of calendars and the events in them. It reads events to show them in Obsidian and creates, updates or deletes events when the owner changes the matching tasks in Obsidian.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar data is processed only on the owner's own computer by the TaskNotes plugin. OAuth tokens are stored locally in Obsidian's secret storage on that computer. The app has no server, and no data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the calendar in TaskNotes, or from the Google Account page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through github.com/rollashinalk.
