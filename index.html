Personal Calendar Sync

Personal Calendar Sync is a private OAuth client used by a single person to connect their own Google Calendar to their own Obsidian vault through the TaskNotes community plugin.

It lets the TaskNotes plugin, running locally in Obsidian on the owner's computer, read and update events in the owner's Google Calendar so that tasks and calendar events stay in sync.

This app is not a public service. It is not offered to other users, and it has no server of its own: all data moves directly between the owner's computer and Google.
